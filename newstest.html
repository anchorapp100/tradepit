NEWSTEST

a sister experiment to the pit: can a blinded AI predict what a news story means for price — before the outcome exists?
How it works. A news article goes in; its keywords are extracted by machine; two independent AI analysts (Gemini via API, Grok via CLI — both with web access disabled, so they cannot look up the ending) each produce a locked, timestamped prediction: direction over 24h and 7d, expected magnitude, confidence. Only after locking is the real price revealed and the prediction graded. CLEAN cases are dated after every model's training — the outcome was genuinely unknowable. HINDSIGHT-RISK cases predate that bar: the model may simply remember what happened, so they test the pipeline, never skill, and the two are never mixed. What survives here shapes what the pit's event ledger will eventually be allowed to whisper to the panel. Predictions run on the operator's local testbed; this page is the published results ledger.
