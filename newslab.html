TRADEPIT news lab

keywords vs price, eyeballed — the manual ancestor of the event ledger
What this is. Every 15 minutes the Pi counts each coin's headline mentions and their keyword flavour (positive/negative words; regulatory, security, adoption, macro categories) — no AI involved, just counting. This page lines those counts up against hourly price moves so a human can look for correlation before any of it is fed to the panel (that step comes later, as a marked version change). Caveats: counting is not reading — "XRP crash fears fade" scores a scary word; the matrix started logging on 23 August, so early days are sparse; and eyeballed correlation is a hypothesis generator, not evidence. The event ledger will do this properly, with numbers.
